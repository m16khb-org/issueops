# Omo task residency, concurrency, and queue fairness

Retrieval date: **2026-10-02**. Scope: official public material, installed package provenance, and issueops architecture; no execution benchmark. Model: `gpt-6.1-sol` (`PI_MODEL`).

## Evidence and findings

**1. Canonical project and released baseline are identifiable.** Installed `$HOME/node_modules/omo-ai/package.json:2-25` identifies `omo-ai` **5.1.8**, repository `code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent`, and engine dependency `@code-yeongyu/senpi` **2026.10.1-2**. The [official release API](https://api.github.com/repos/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/releases/tags/v5.1.8) records publication at **2026-10-01T15:00:33Z**; the [version-pinned changelog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/v5.1.8/CHANGELOG.md) labels 5.1.8 October 1 and names that engine. Findings below describe this released baseline, not an anticipated version. Certainty: high for identity/version. Counterevidence: installed metadata does not establish byte-for-byte equivalence with upstream source. Applicability: bind issueops observations to both wrapper and engine versions.

**2. Running capacity has lane limits plus a separate global budget.** [Released allocator source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/v5.1.8/packages/senpi-task/src/manager/concurrency.ts), lines **52-66, 255-276**, resolves model override before provider override before default; default lane capacity is five. Provider overrides group models into a provider lane unless a model override wins. Zero means unlimited. Even unlimited lanes consume global leases. [Official configuration reference](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/v5.1.8/docs/reference/omo-json.md), lines **362-367, 383**, documents effective global default `max(8, availableParallelism() * 2)`, per senpi process, not machine-wide; OpenCode background tasks are unaffected. Certainty: high for allocator behavior; effective-default claim is official documentation. Counterevidence: the allocator alone treats omitted global capacity as unlimited; its constructor is not the effective configuration boundary. Applicability: expose resolved capacity and process identity before diagnosing slow admission.

**3. Queue fairness exceeds per-lane FIFO but is not universal FIFO.** Allocator lines **107-119, 217-252** append a monotonically increasing sequence, inspect each lane's head, skip capacity-blocked lanes, and grant the oldest eligible head across lanes. Lines **74-80** prevent same-lane newcomers bypassing that lane's queue. [Released tests](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/v5.1.8/packages/senpi-task/src/manager/concurrency.test.ts), lines **368-388, 410-416**, cover oldest-eligible selection, blocked-lane bypass, and same-lane anti-barging. Certainty: high by source inspection; tests were read, not executed. Counterevidence: no parent identity or weighted share participates in selection, and direct acquisition checks only its own lane's queue. This is not proof of cross-parent proportional fairness or starvation freedom. Applicability: report queue position as lane-local, alongside age and blocking reason.

**4. Resuming owners deliberately outrank new queue heads.** Allocator lines **140-168, 217-228** park a held lease, release its capacity, then prioritize eligible resumable owners before queued starts. `unpark(..., { overflow: true })` resumes immediately without ordinary capacity checks. Released tests lines **95-107** explicitly expect two occupants under a one-slot limit during promotion overflow. Epoch-keyed leases and stale-token checks prevent old releases or parking tokens from reviving later ownership. Certainty: high. Counterevidence: these are concurrency-lease parking rules, not terminal-child residency reclamation; calling both simply "parking" hides different resources. Applicability: visibility should separate held, parked, resumable, queued, and temporary overflow counts. A hard-cap assertion would contradict the tested exception.

**5. Residency policy changed recently and has an independent idle lifecycle.** The pinned changelog records **5.1.0, 2026-09-28**, changing `task.residency_max_children` to `"unlimited"` by default; numbered bounds remain supported. The configuration reference documents oldest finished idle eviction at a configured cap. [Official task guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/v5.1.8/docs/guide/senpi-task.md), lines **58-62**, specifies a **900000 ms** idle timeout, sweep-at-or-after expiry, protection for running/pending-steering children, and reversible `persisted_only`/`rpc_detached` parking. The [5.0.0-beta.68 release](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/releases/tag/v5.0.0-beta.68), dated **2026-09-16** in the installed changelog, reports fixing a second DAG run's false residency denial. Certainty: high for documented policy; historical defect is an official single-source claim. Counterevidence: unlimited residency does not mean unlimited running capacity or permanent retention. Applicability: issueops must distinguish admission pressure from retained child resources.
